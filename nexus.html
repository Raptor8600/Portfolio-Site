About Nexus Research

Nexus Research is an independent equity analysis series by Mary Scott, applying a systematic driver-based framework to identify variant views and mispriced risk in U.S. public equities. Each report isolates revenue, demand, margin, and structural drivers, surfaces the key market consensus being challenged, and identifies catalysts with explicit falsification criteria.

All views are personal and for educational purposes only. Nothing in these reports constitutes investment advice. Mary Scott is not a registered financial advisor.
